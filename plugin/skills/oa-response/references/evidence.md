# Evidence

See bundled `evidence-rules.md` (copied here at skill-build time).
Primary-source verification of load-bearing propositions is mandatory.
Full ingestion of the specification and every reference is not.

## Figures

A figure record is the specification's description of a figure, not the
drawing. Open the sheet itself (`evidence_read` on the drawing/sheet
`pkb://` address) when the examiner cites a figure, when a cited paragraph
says "as shown in FIG. X", when the mapped limitation is structural or
spatial, or when assessing §112 support for an amendment (the applicant's
own drawings are support). If no sheet is held, say the drawing was not
verified — do not argue from the brief description as though it were the
figure.
