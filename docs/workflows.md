# Patent prosecution workflows, skills, and MCP tools

Work with your AI as you would with a prepared patent associate: establish
the record, inspect the evidence, compare strategies, and approve the next
step. PatentReply supplies the indexed matter and the workflow state across
those conversations.

## How a response develops

1. **Orient and audit.** Compile or open the matter, identify its posture,
   and inspect the record's coverage and integrity findings.
2. **Review.** Identify the live rejections and objections; read the claim
   language, cited reference passages, and relevant authority.
3. **Develop a plan.** Compare per-issue response strategies and identify
   specification support for proposed amendments.
4. **Approve.** Submit a specific plan revision for the registered
   practitioner's approval on the PatentReply approval page.
5. **Draft and assemble.** Use the approved revision for the response and
   claim listing generated by the hosted service.
6. **Review the draft.** Check record support, citations, formatting, and
   argument quality before the practitioner finalizes the filing.

A change of strategy after approval requires a revised plan and a new
approval. The [Office Action skill](../plugin/skills/oa-response/SKILL.md)
defines the workflow and recovery rules.

## Tool overview

These examples come from the bundled workflow files. This is a capability
map, not an exhaustive API specification; your connection's tool definitions
provide the available parameters and current behavior.

| Capability | Representative tools | What the AI uses them for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Build and open the matter | `matter_compile`, `matter_refresh`, `matter_open` | Obtain or update the compiled prosecution record. |
| Inspect record quality | `matter_audit` | Surface coverage gaps, integrity findings, and disputed claim text. |
| Read claims and prosecution documents | `claims_get`, `matter_read` | Retrieve operative claim text and source documents. |
| Analyze the Office Action | `oa_review` | Inspect grounds, affected claims, cited evidence, and related review surfaces. |
| Verify evidence | `evidence_search`, `evidence_read`, `evidence_resolve` | Locate and read the passages supporting an analysis. |
| Retrieve authority | `authority_read`, `authority_resolve` | Obtain supported legal-authority text. |
| Plan and track the response | `oa_response_status`, `oa_response_plan` | Read workflow state and develop a response plan. |
| Assemble an approved response | `oa_amendment_listing`, `oa_response_document` | Produce the claim listing and Word response through the server. |
| Review a supplied draft | `matter_upload_request`, `oa_response_qc` | Attach a response draft and support record-based QC. |

The default response workflow works from the prosecution record and the
references of record. A separate prior-art search is a distinct task.

## Reading and displaying sources

The AI reads evidence through retrieval tools. When you want to inspect a
claim, paragraph, figure, or authority yourself, ask it to show that item.
Source viewers and `pkb://` addresses connect the explanation to the stored
record; access depends on the signed-in matter and client support.

See the [FAQ](faq.md) for public records, uploads, and the scope of QC.
